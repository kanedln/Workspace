About Kane

Educator first.
Consultant second.
Optimist always.

I spent a decade in classrooms before I ever called myself a consultant. That's because the best ideas in education aren't in white papers — they're in the kid who finally cracks a word in their second language, the teacher who stays ten minutes late to translate a flyer, the principal who actually shows up to family night.

Now I help districts and schools turn those moments into systems — through program design, professional development, coaching, and the occasional bad pun in two languages.

How It Works

A four-step recipe for actually moving the needle.

I'm not in the business of dropping reports and disappearing. Here's how a typical engagement unfolds.

Step 01

Listen.

I sit in classrooms, talk to teachers, listen to families, and find out where the actual friction is.

Step 02

Diagnose.

Together we name what's working, what's broken, and what's just been quietly accepted for too long.

Step 03

Build.

Plans, PD, coaching cycles — whatever it takes to move from idea to Monday morning.

Step 04

Hand off.

My favorite part: when your team no longer needs me. I'm a partner, not a permanent expense.
